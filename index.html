04.1

Zakazhi AI Prototype

An AI phone receptionist for private clinics in North Macedonia. It answers calls 24/7 in Macedonian, Albanian and English, takes appointment requests, and sends them to the clinic's receptionist to approve with one tap before calling the patient back to confirm.

  • Clinic dashboard with call log, live-call view, appointments and a day/week/month calendar with drag-and-drop.
  • Approval flow over WhatsApp and Viber, plus three scheduling modes, including support for clinics still using a paper appointment book.
  • Full interface in three languages and a separate platform-owner area for revenue and clinic management.
  • Built around clear rules: AI disclosure on every call, no medical data stored, and humans make the final booking decision.

React, Vite, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, i18n (MK / SQ / EN), voice AI
